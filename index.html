Hydrone

The Brazilian United Team for Intelligent Automation - FBOT@Hydrone was founded in 2018 by FBOT from Universidade Federal do Rio Grande (FURG). The team FBOT@Hydrone has already taken part in several national and international robotics competitions, achieving prominent results such as the 1st place in the Brazilian Robotics Competition (CBR) - Flying Robots Trial League in 2019. Besides, we made history by becoming the first Brazilian team to compete in the drone category of the FIRA RoboWorld Cup, held in Wolfenbüttel, Germany in 2023, where we secured the 4th position worldwide in the "Air-Emergency Service Indoor" category.
